# Vanilla-style small components

The `base/` PNGs are the original 16x16 Parts & Circuits item sprites.
Run `python tools/vanilla_style_items.py` from the repository root to rebuild
the 28 small component icons and the enlarged `preview.png` contact sheet.

The Oil & Chemistry item sprites retain their earlier appearance and are
generated separately by `tools/oil_assets.py`.

Metal rods and plates are generated by `python tools/metal_stock_textures.py`.
They share the exact Nichrome rod and plate silhouettes and shade placement,
recoloured with each metal's ingot palette. That script also updates their
heat-glow variants and item definitions.

Keep native 16x16 pixels, crisp edges, a small material-specific palette,
and a readable inventory silhouette.
