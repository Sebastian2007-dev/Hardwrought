# Tier-1 compendium concept prompt

Create a production-ready pixel-art UI concept for the first, primitive Hardwrought compendium.
It is improvised from overlapping leaves, bark scraps, thin twigs and coarse plant-fibre lashings,
not a polished leather book. Show it fully open as a compact two-page spread. Put two pale,
low-noise rag-paper scraps inside as readable writing surfaces. Keep every control physically
inside the object: back, previous/next page, search, category tabs and method bookmarks. Method
bookmarks use recognizable Minecraft-style item icons (crafting table, chest, campfire, smithing
table, furnace and log). Unknown entries are solid black silhouettes of their actual items, never a
generic question-mark cube. Use crisp hard pixel edges, earthy olive/bark/charcoal colours, strong
contrast and sparse texture beneath text. No title banner, no floating controls, no glossy fantasy
ornament, no tiny illegible text. The later upgraded compendium may become the polished layered
book shown in the supplied reference, but this image must look handmade on the first survival day.
