# Hardwrought

Hardwrought is a hardcore survival and progression overhaul for Fabric. It starts you with bare hands and takes you through iron, bronze, steel, magic, electricity and oil, then on into a harder Nether, a new Underworld dimension and a reworked End, and finally to a ritual that ends the game. Hunger, thirst, temperature, wounds and illness all matter. Metal tools are forged or cast, not crafted. You won't find a guide on a wiki: the in-game **Compendium** has the recipes for what you have found and notes that point you to the next step without giving the answer away.

## Features

### Survival basics
- Calories, hydration, stamina, fatigue, carrying weight and body temperature, all tracked on the server
- Four seasons that shift each biome by a different amount; clothing, fire and wet clothes all count
- Water is finite and has a quality: rivers, seawater, swamp water, groundwater and rain. Boil it or filter it before you drink
- Wounds, infections, colds, stomach bugs and burns, treated with bandages, herbal teas and later medicine
- A hurt body is slower, and fights wear you down until your wounds heal
- Stone, ore and wood need a tool. You start with flint, fibre and fire made by friction
- A taller world (Y −256 to 1023) with thin air on high peaks, geothermal heat deep down and large ore deposits you find by prospecting

### Metal and smithing
- Ore is heated and hammered into bars at the anvil, then forged into heads and blades
- Forging quality and heat treatment (quenching, tempering) carry over into the finished tool
- Workbenches come in stages: the first is hewn from a standing log, and a better one is nailed together with bronze
- Weapon classes and damage types (cut, pierce, blunt), blocking and parrying

### Workshop, smeltery and steel
- Crushers, hand cranks, water wheels and windmills on a mechanical driveline
- A multiblock smeltery built from refractory brick, with alloying, faucets and casts
- Steel, stainless steel, titanium and tungsten steel. Plain steel rusts

### Magic and runes
- Runes drawn with the mouse, combined by a grammar into spells, cast with wands that carry a limited load
- Sigils: geometric constructions laid on the ground
- Enchanting by writing runes onto the piece with lapis powder

### Electricity, oil and automation
- A simulated electric network: dynamos, lines, masts, batteries, transformers and a 240 V grid
- Electric machines, item and fluid pipes, the assembler and a Tesla tower
- Oil wells, a refinery column, diesel generators and gasoline engines

### The Nether
- Steel is needed to get in, and the first crossing is unstable. You arrive with nothing and start over
- The Ash Grove biome, Nether ores and portal stabilisers to get home

### The Wither
- Soul-charged skulls, and a Wither with new powers

### The Underworld and the Witch King
- A new cave dimension below the abyss, with its own biomes and ruins
- Mithril and adamantium
- The Witch King, a magic boss

### The End and the Void Dragon
- A broken island over the void, and a fight in the air
- A dragon with its own AI that can be raised again

### The final ritual
- Build a temple, gather eight offerings and complete the ritual that ends the game

### The Compendium
- **Knowledge:** a recipe and usage browser (`R` / `U`) that names only what you have discovered. Loot and mob drops are included
- **Thoughts:** a journal in twelve chapters, from *Bare Hands* to *The Ritual*. Every chapter has optional "closer look" sub-chapters with the exact rules
- Also: an atlas that maps the land you have walked through and lets you mark places

## Ultra mode

Choose **Ultra** on the world-creation screen, next to the game mode, for **Ultra Survival** or **Ultra Hardcore**:

- No starting pack. Your inventory opens up one row at a time as you make better packs
- Difficulty is set to Hard and locked
- No natural regeneration. Only effects (such as potions or golden apples) or a sleep above 75 % quality heal you
- Nothing breaks instantly: at least 3 seconds by hand, even for grass and flowers, and at least 0.5 seconds with the right tool
- No 40-minute grace period of immunity to illness at the start or after death

## Requirements

- Minecraft **26.3**
- Fabric Loader **0.19.5** or newer
- Fabric API
- Java **25**
- Sodium is supported

## Important: new world required

Hardwrought changes world generation in the Overworld, the Nether and the End and adds a new Underworld dimension. **Start a new world.** Existing worlds are not supported.

## Known issues

- This is the first public release
- Multiplayer has been tested less than singleplayer
- Balance may change between versions
